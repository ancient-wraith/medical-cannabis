# Reviewing My Medical Cannabis Results

This page is a reusable template for reviewing my medical cannabis diary entries and summarising my experiences with different flowers.

I want to identify possible patterns in how each flower affects my **depression, mood, anxiety, bone pain, nerve pain and sleep**, including any effects that continue into the following day.

### How to use this template

- [ ] Copy this template whenever I want to review a flower or a period of treatment.
- [ ] Base my observations on my diary entries rather than a single session.
- [ ] Record both perceived benefits and unwanted effects.
- [ ] Leave sections blank if I don't have enough information.
- [ ] Discuss significant findings or concerns with my prescribing clinician.

My observations are personal experiences, not proof that a product caused a particular effect.
