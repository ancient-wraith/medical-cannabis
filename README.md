# Medical Cannabis Research

This repository documents my personal research into prescribed medical cannabis and its potential effects on my mental and physical wellbeing.

My aim is to understand how different medical cannabis flowers affect my symptoms, identify patterns in my experiences, and evaluate which products may be most suitable for daytime and evening use.

My research focuses on **bone pain, nerve pain, anxiety, depression and seasonal affective disorder (SAD)**.

I want to consider not only symptom relief but also mood, motivation, concentration, sleep, unwanted effects and how I feel the following day.

## Research Goals

- Understand how different flowers affect my mental and physical health.
- Monitor changes in depression, mood and anxiety.
- Evaluate relief from bone pain and nerve pain.
- Explore effects on motivation, enjoyment and concentration.
- Record sleep quality and potential next-day effects.
- Compare subjective effects and unwanted side effects.
- Assess product quality, including appearance, smell, taste, texture and vapour production.
- Compare THC and CBD content, product characteristics and cost.
- Maintain consistent records to help identify patterns over time.
- Use my findings to support informed discussions with my prescribing clinician.

## Results

The [`results/`](results/) folder contains my individual medical cannabis product reviews and observations.

Each review uses a consistent format to help compare products and track experiences over time.

Reviews may include:

- **Product details:** name, THC/CBD content, irradiation status and cost.
- **Dosage and timing:** when taken, dosage and method of administration.
- **Mental health:** depression, mood, anxiety, motivation and enjoyment.
- **Physical health:** bone pain, nerve pain and other physical symptoms.
- **Subjective effects:** changes in relaxation, sociability, mood and perception.
- **Unwanted effects:** dizziness, light-headed, sleepiness, anxiety and other adverse experiences.
- **Sleep and next-day effects:** sleep quality, pain relief, mood and daytime functioning.
- **Product quality:** appearance, smell, taste, texture and vapour production.
- **Overall assessment:** perceived benefits, unwanted effects and value for money.

These records are intended to help me understand my individual responses and make more informed comparisons between products.

## Research Approach

My approach is based on recording personal observations consistently rather than relying solely on product descriptions or strain classifications.

Where possible, I aim to:

- Record symptoms and wellbeing in relation to treatment.
- Distinguish immediate effects from those experienced the following day.
- Consider both benefits and unwanted effects.
- Record product details and costs to support meaningful comparisons.
- Look for recurring patterns across multiple observations.
- Recognise that sleep, stress, activity and other factors may influence my observations.
- Discuss significant findings or concerns with my prescribing clinician.

Indica, Sativa and Hybrid classifications are recorded as product descriptors, not reliable predictors of individual effects. THC/CBD percentages and terpene profiles may provide useful context, but they cannot guarantee a particular response.

My observations represent personal experiences and do not establish that a particular product caused a change in symptoms.

## Disclaimer

This repository is for **research purposes only**. It documents my personal experiences with prescribed medical cannabis in relation to bone pain, nerve pain, anxiety, depression and seasonal affective disorder (SAD).

The information is not medical advice and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical guidance.

Individual responses to medical cannabis vary. Potential benefits, side effects and impairment should be considered when evaluating any product.

Product availability, specifications and prices may change and should be verified with the prescribing clinic or dispensing pharmacy.

Any changes to prescribed medication or dosage should be discussed with my prescribing clinician.
