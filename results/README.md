# Medical Cannabis Results

This directory contains personal reviews and observations of prescribed medical cannabis products. The records are intended to make it easier to compare products consistently and notice patterns in how they relate to symptoms, wellbeing, sleep, and day-to-day functioning.

## Overview

Individual result files document experiences with specific products. Depending on the review, records may include product details (such as THC/CBD content and cost), dose and timing, perceived effects on mental and physical symptoms, sleep and next-day functioning, unwanted effects, and product quality.

Use each individual review for the observations and context specific to that product.

## What the Results Mean

The results describe **personal, subjective experiences**, not clinical trial findings. They can help identify possible patterns—for example, whether a product was associated with perceived changes in pain, anxiety, mood, motivation, concentration, sleep, or unwanted effects.

- **Perceived benefits** describe changes noticed during or after use.
- **Unwanted effects** record negative or difficult experiences and should be considered alongside any perceived benefits.
- **Dose and timing** provide context for interpreting an observation.
- **Sleep and next-day notes** help distinguish immediate experiences from effects noticed later.
- **THC/CBD content, terpenes, and product characteristics** provide product context but cannot reliably predict an individual's response.
- **Cost and product-quality notes** support practical comparisons, but do not establish effectiveness.

A result should not be read as proof that a product caused a change. Symptoms and effects may also be influenced by sleep, stress, activity, other medicines, and other factors.

## Comparing Products

When comparing reviews, consider the whole record rather than a single rating or observation. Look for patterns across multiple occasions, taking account of dose, timing, symptom severity, and unwanted effects. Incomplete or differently recorded observations may not be directly comparable.

## Limitations

These records are personal observations and are not a substitute for controlled research or medical advice. Individual responses vary, and observations alone cannot establish safety, effectiveness, or causation. Product details and availability may change.

Discuss significant effects or concerns, and any proposed changes to prescribed treatment or dosage, with the prescribing clinician. Do not use these records alone to make treatment decisions.

## Using These Records

Browse the individual product reviews in this directory and use the accompanying notes to understand the context of each observation. The findings are intended to support reflection and informed conversations with the prescribing clinician, not to recommend a product for other people.
